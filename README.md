# 🛒 Shop Track Sales & Operations

## 📊 Project Overview

Shop Track Sales & Operations is an Excel-based data analysis project focused on analyzing sales performance, customer activity, product performance, regional revenue, and order operations.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights using data cleaning, PivotTables, calculations, and an interactive Excel dashboard.

The analysis focuses on revenue, orders, quantity sold, customer performance, product categories, regional performance, monthly sales trends, and order status.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify the regions generating the highest revenue
- Evaluate product and product category performance
- Identify the top customers by revenue
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Examine order status distribution
- Calculate key sales performance indicators
- Transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights
- Develop an interactive Excel dashboard for decision-making

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The original dataset contained raw and inconsistent sales records that required cleaning and preparation before analysis.

### Data Cleaning Activities

The following activities were carried out using Microsoft Excel:

- Reviewed the dataset for missing and blank values
- Checked for duplicate records
- Corrected inconsistent date formats
- Reviewed and corrected inconsistent quantity values
- Checked numerical fields for accuracy and consistency
- Reviewed Unit Price, Total Sales, Discount, and Net Sales
- Standardized categorical fields such as Product, Category, Region, City, Payment Method, and Order Status
- Reviewed discount values for consistency
- Organized the cleaned data into an Excel Table
- Prepared the dataset for PivotTable analysis and dashboard development

## 💡 Key Insights

### Revenue Performance

- The business generated a total revenue of **$750.86 million** across **612 orders**.
- The average order value was approximately **$1.23 million**, indicating a high average transaction value within the dataset.

### Regional Performance

- Revenue varied across the five regions: Central, East, North, South, and West.
- The regional analysis provides insight into the strongest-performing markets and areas with opportunities for improvement.

### Product Performance

- Revenue varied considerably across the products analyzed.
- Laptops and cameras were among the stronger revenue-generating products shown on the dashboard.

### Customer Performance

- Revenue was concentrated among a group of high-value customers.
- The Top 10 Customer analysis helps identify customers who make significant contributions to overall revenue.

### Monthly Sales Trend

- Monthly revenue fluctuated throughout the year.
- The monthly trend provides a clearer view of periods of stronger and weaker sales performance.

### Order Operations

- Orders were distributed across Completed, Pending, Cancelled, and Refunded statuses.
- Monitoring order status can help identify operational challenges and opportunities to improve order fulfillment.
